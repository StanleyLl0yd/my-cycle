# My Cycle Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026.

My Cycle is an offline period diary for Android. The app does not require an account and does not transmit the user's diary records to the developer or third parties.

## Data processed by the app

Users may store period dates, bleeding intensity, mood, symptoms, and free-form notes. This health-related information is processed and stored only on the user's device.

App settings, including the selected cycle situation and appearance settings, are also stored only on the device.

## Data collection and sharing

My Cycle does not send data to a developer server and does not use analytics, advertising, trackers, or cloud synchronization. The app's Android manifest does not request the `INTERNET` permission.

The app does not sell or share user data with third parties.

## Export

Users may explicitly export diary records as CSV. The destination is chosen through Android's system file picker. Export happens only when the user requests it; My Cycle does not upload the CSV to a developer server.

## Backup

Android app-data backup is disabled. App files, the local database, and settings are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-transfer extraction rules.

## Deletion

All diary records and app settings can be deleted from My Cycle settings. Deletion happens locally on the device.

## Medical information

My Cycle is a diary, not a medical device, diagnostic tool, or substitute for professional medical advice. Calendar predictions are approximate and must not be used as a method of contraception.
