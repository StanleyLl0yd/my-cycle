## Purpose

My Cycle is a personal diary for recording menstrual-cycle history. It stores user-entered records and shows approximate calendar estimates based on recorded dates.

The app is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, or contraceptive method. It does not diagnose conditions, determine an actual ovulation day, or replace professional medical advice.

## User responsibility

Users decide what information to enter and how to use the information shown by the app. Calendar estimates must not be treated as a guarantee of menstruation, ovulation, pregnancy, or the absence of pregnancy.

If symptoms cause concern, users should seek advice from a qualified medical professional.

## Data and privacy

Data handling is described in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). Records remain on the device unless the user explicitly exports them as CSV.
